How to Play

  • Swipe up, down, left, or right to move the number blocks.
  • Blocks with the same number merge when they collide.
  • Keep merging blocks to create higher numbers.
  • The game ends when there are no valid moves left.

Game Progress

Your score increases as you merge blocks. Try to beat your best score and reach the highest possible number.

Game Controls

Mobile: Swipe in any direction to move the blocks.

Desktop: Use the Arrow Keys or WASD keys & mouse controls.

Game Rules & Mechanics

1. Swipe or press Arrow Keys / WASD to move blocks in four directions.

2. Matching adjacent blocks merge into a block of double value.

3. Reach the target block tile to complete the level!
